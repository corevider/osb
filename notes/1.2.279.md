# Beast-form resurrection is accepted, and never leaves you dead
- With "Accept resurrection from party members" on, the bot now also answers Yes to a party member's beast-form resurrection ("Someone is trying to resurrect you in a beast form"). Before, it ignored that question.
- New switch under it: "Also accept beast-form resurrection", on by default. Beast form means weaker basic attacks, more defence and much less HP and MP. Turn it off to answer that question yourself.
- Fixed: while an unanswered beast-form question was open, the bot also never revived itself, so the character stayed dead. Now an offer the bot leaves alone no longer holds back its own revive. With "Accept resurrection" off, open offers are still left for you to answer, as before.
> Tested with the integration suite in simulation, not in a live game.
