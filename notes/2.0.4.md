# Resurrection from anyone, resurrecting helpers outside the party, zero-setup leveling

- New on the Party page: "Accept resurrection from any player" (off by default). When you are dead, the bot also answers Yes to a Rebirth from a player outside your party. The log names who resurrected you. With it on, any living player in view counts as a helper for "Wait for a party resurrection".
- New on the Party page: "Also resurrect these players (not in the party)". Enter up to 20 character names, for example a lure character helping a full party from outside it. A listed player who is dead, in view and within the maximum distance is resurrected like a party member, during hunting and while stopped. Nobody else outside the party is targeted. If the game refuses Rebirth on one of them, the bot says so once and skips that player for 10 minutes.
- "Max every skill" (Settings, Auto mastery) is now a switch. While it is on, it spends new skill points as you get them. Off by default.
- New on the Setup page: zero-setup leveling, "Level up by itself" (off by default). The bot picks a hunting spot for your level from built-in game data, buys the next tier of NPC weapon, armour and accessories on town trips, and can raise your weapon's mastery, learn its skills and spend stat points. Seal and rare gear, +4 and higher, and anything you protect are never replaced, and a gold reserve is never spent.
- Auto mastery has a new choice under "Skills to upgrade": "Every skill of my chosen masteries", which also learns new skills.
- The Update button can be clicked again in the compact window, the classic window keeps one size on every tab, and the wide window splits its boxes evenly.
