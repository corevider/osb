# Gold amounts with dots
- Gold fields now show dots between thousands, like 100.000.000, so big amounts are easy to read.
- You can type them with dots, commas or spaces, or short: 500k, 20m (or 20kk), 1.5b.
- Used for the alchemy buying limits, the leveling gold to keep and stall prices. The alchemy log shows gold the same way.
> Tested in simulation, not in a live game.
