# The pickup pet keeps collecting while you play by hand, resurrection retry settings

- New in Loot → Collection: "Pet keeps collecting while I play by hand" (on by default). When you walk, attack or use skills yourself, the pickup pet now keeps collecting, with the bot running or stopped. Only the pet moves; your character's own pickups and hunting still pause after manual control. Turn it off to have the pet wait as before.
- Your own inventory use goes first. A pet pickup keeps the game's inventory busy until the pet reaches the drop, and meanwhile the game greys out its inventory and Item Mall buttons and quietly ignores a purchase. Now the pet sends no new pickup for 2.5 s after you use or move an item, pick something up or use a shop, storage, trade, stall or alchemy, and for 60 s after you open or use the Item Mall. It also waits while a trade, stall or alchemy window is open, and leaves a short gap after each pickup so your clicks get through. The bot's own item drops, pet-to-bag moves and Item Mall buying wait the same way. The bot never repeats a command of yours, so a purchase is never sent twice.
- A drop the pet cannot reach is now given up after a few tries instead of being tried again and again.
- The pet status line shows how much of the last minute pet pickups kept the inventory busy. With detailed capture on, the Activity log shows how long each pet pickup took. These numbers stay on your computer.
- New on the Party page: "Wait between resurrection attempts (s)" (31 s by default, the game's 30 s answer window plus one tick; 31 to 300) and "Max resurrection attempts per player (0 = no limit)". After the last attempt the bot stops offering Rebirth to that player and says so once; it starts counting again when the player is alive again, leaves the party or the list, or when you press Start.
