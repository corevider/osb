# The lure casts its taunts at your pull points

- The lure now casts both taunts at each pull point you saved: the area taunt first, then the single-target taunt on the monster nearest the point. The area taunt is no longer cast on the way to the point, on the way back or at the training spot.
- If a taunt is still cooling down when the character arrives, it waits at the point for up to 10 s; when the area taunt is still not ready after that, the single-target taunt goes out without it. A point with no monster sends the lure straight back.
- On the way back, the single-target taunt is cast again on a monster pulled at this point that falls more than 10 m behind and is within the skill's range, at most once per 10 s for each monster, and never when hurrying back on low HP. The lure never turns round for it.
