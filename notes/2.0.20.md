# An alchemy queue, OSB live, and fixes for reconnects and resurrection

- New **Alchemy queue**: add several items, each with its own Plus, White and Blue goals, and **Start queue** works through them in order. Blue (magic) stones are new. Each item picks its own elixir, and shields, armour and accessories use their own elixirs. Queued items stay in the bag on town trips, and hunting, the unique hunt and other jobs wait while the queue runs.
- **Buy what the queue needs (spends gold)**, off by default: the queue buys its elixirs, powders and stones at the Jangan alchemy merchant until the stones are done, within the stock, spend and keep limits under **Buy at the Jangan alchemy merchant**.
- An alchemy attempt the server never confirmed no longer needs a page reload. Gold amounts show with dots.
- **Reconnect:** when the game stays on connecting, entering the world or reconnecting for 3 minutes with no error, the page reloads. When a walk never gets the game's collision data, the page reloads after 3 minutes; after 5 such reloads in a row the bot stops reloading for this and says so.
- **Party resurrection:** while the bot runs with Revive in town on, it now declines a resurrection when town is needed (no return scroll, no gold, broken or worn gear, a chosen potion used up) and revives in town instead (**Revive in town instead when town is needed**, on by default). While stopped it keeps accepting. Beast-form resurrection is accepted only with **Also accept beast-form resurrection** (off by default), and an offer the bot leaves alone no longer keeps the character dead.
- **Full bag:** on arrival in town a warning says when the bag is nearly full and nothing in it is sold or stored, and the final stop says which setting fixes it.
- **Buff scrolls:** keeps the 2-minute Damage Increase, Damage Absorption, HP 1000 and MP 1000 scrolls active, and new switches (off by default) keep HP, MP, Hit and Dodging scrolls active.
- **OSB live**, a separate and optional phone dashboard, is in the repository's `live/` folder. It is local only unless you start it with `--phone`; the bot itself sends nothing more than before. See `live/README.md`.
- Tested in simulation only: the alchemy queue and buying, the resurrection decline and beast form, both reloads and the new scroll IDs have not been checked on the live server.
