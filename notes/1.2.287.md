# Shields, armour and accessories get their own elixirs
- Fixed: a shield, armour piece or accessory in the alchemy queue could keep the weapon elixir picked earlier. Then no elixir of its own kind was bought, and its plus step found no elixir.
- Now each item always uses and buys elixirs of its own kind. An elixir choice that does not fit the item counts as "any elixir of its kind", in queues you already saved too.
> Tested in simulation, not in a live game.
