# A Unique tab, and a unique hunt that searches without stalling

- New **Unique** tab right after **Setup**, in the compact, wide and classic windows. It holds everything about uniques: the **Unique hunt** card with Start and Stop, the **Uniques** list, the Unique hunt settings with the nest patrol, the chime for a unique coming into view, and the safety trigger **When a unique appears nearby**. The Combat **Unique hunt** sub-tab and the unique cards on the Setup page are gone. The Unique and Titan rows of Monster classes, the Berserk ticks and the Party assist ticks stay where they were. Your settings are unchanged.
- **Fight monsters that hit me** now applies only once the unique hunt has found a unique, until it goes back to patrolling. While it searches, monsters that hit you are ignored and the patrol walks on, so a busy area no longer keeps it from finding the unique. A unique that hits you is still fought at once. The new tick **Also while searching** (off by default) brings back the old behaviour.
- **Patrol the game's unique nests** is now on by default. A saved profile that had it off and no patrol spots of its own is switched on once; turning it off afterwards sticks.
- The unique tracker, if you turned it on, is now read while the Unique tab is open instead of the Setup page.
- **Zero-setup leveling** moved to the bottom of the Setup page.
