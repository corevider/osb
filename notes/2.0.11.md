# Town trips skip the potion merchant when the supplies are full

- The bot remembers, for each character and potion merchant, which HP and MP potion size the merchant sells and which HGP potions and pet kits it does not sell. This stays in your browser after a reload and is never put in your profile, share codes or backups.
- With full supplies, the town trip now skips the potion merchant also on the first trip after a reload, and the log says "Town: nothing to do at the potion merchant; skipped." Before, the bot counted X-Large potions after a reload, so it walked to the merchant for nothing and could even start a town trip for potions it already had.
- A potion merchant the bot has not visited yet is visited once to learn what it sells. Saving the potion merchant again, or the automatic town setup, forgets what was learned about it. With fewer than 2 return scrolls in the bag, the trip still visits the merchant.
