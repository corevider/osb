# Weapon swaps for buffs no longer get stuck

- Buffs with **Swap weapons for buffs**: a buff that cannot be cast after the weapon change (cooldown, not enough MP, the game refuses it, or a party member out of range or out of view) now waits two minutes, and the log says which buff and why. Before, the bot changed weapons again and again every few seconds and stopped attacking.
- Your primary weapon is never given up for good. If the game refuses it, for example right after you enter the game while your pet is still picking up loot, OSB tries again after 30 seconds and then every five minutes, and entering the game starts over. Before, two quick refusals made OSB keep the other weapon, so attack skills that need your primary weapon were refused.
- Your own buffs are cast in the order of your buff list, and a buff that is still running is renewed sooner after the weapon change.
- Fixed an error with buffs that need a shield when OSB picks the weapon by itself.
- A merged pull request is no longer released by itself: new versions come from the maintainers.
