# The pickup pet leaves you room, the Unique hunt knows which uniques are alive, live uniques on Setup

- With the bot stopped, the pickup pet no longer keeps your inventory locked. It waits 3 s after any click, key or scroll in the game (not in the OSB window), takes only drops within 25 m of it, keeps at most about a third of any 10 seconds for itself, and waits until a return scroll, reverse return scroll or teleport has finished. Your scrolls, potions and Item Mall work again while it collects in your pauses. New in Loot → Collection: "Pet collects while the bot is stopped" (on), "Pet waits after your clicks and keys (s)" (3) and "Pet reach while the bot is stopped (m)" (25).
- The Unique hunt now stops after killing its unique even when the game did not show the death in the usual way: a unique shown dead, announced as killed, or disappearing at low HP right after your hits counts as your kill. Your own hits during manual control count too.
- The bot remembers which uniques are alive or dead, from the game's announcements, what it sees and its kills. The hunt skips uniques known to be dead; when all chosen uniques are dead it stays where it is ("Waiting: Tiger Girl was killed 12 min ago.") and goes on when one appears again. "Trust a unique's death for (min)" (30) is in Combat → Unique hunt.
- At the game's unique nests the bot now looks around for about 2 seconds and moves on when no unique is there. "Wait at each spot" is for your own spots only and is 0 by default; settings that saved a wait keep it.
- New on the Setup page, under the Unique hunt card: the Uniques list, ordered by level, lowest first, with each state as a coloured pill (Alive green, Slain red, Not up amber, Unknown dim), the map and the nest count. The Hunt ticks choose which uniques the Unique hunt looks for, and ticking one turns on "Patrol the game's unique nests". Click a unique for a small map of its nests and how far the nearest one is.
- The list shows each unique once. OSB knows the 13 uniques by the game's own monster numbers, so a second monster with the same name (Cerberus, Captin Ivy) no longer adds a row or sends the patrol to the wrong spawn points; old ticks and states saved under such a copy move to the real unique. The levels of all 13 are built in, and Lord Yarkan has one more nest, from a player's report.
- New switch, off by default: "Check the game site's unique tracker". When you turn it on, OSB reads opensro.online/uniques at most every 2 minutes while the Setup page is open or the Unique hunt runs, without cookies and without any data about you. Only each unique's state, level and map are read, never who killed it. Until you turn it on, OSB makes no request to the game site for it.
