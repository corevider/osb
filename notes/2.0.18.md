# Party giants, cave follow, party MP heals and a hunting-area sweep

- Party giants, such as the giants of Jangan Cave, are now hunted by default like other giants (Combat → Hunt area → Monster classes → Party giant). Settings saved earlier follow your Giant choice, so a character with Giant on Ignore now also takes party giants. A party giant you set to Prefer or Ignore yourself is kept. To keep a weak character away from them, set Party giant to Avoid.
- Following a party member now works inside a cave.
- Party support can restore party MP: an MP heal skill and a group MP heal skill, each with its own threshold, and **Heal my MP too**. HP heals still come first.
- New **Search the hunting area** option (off by default): with no monster in view, the bot walks the hunting area north, east, west and south through the spot to find monsters beyond view. It stays inside a polygon hunting area and never runs while luring or following.
- The log now names what was picked up, and SoX gear its seal: "Picked up … (Seal of Sun)". Session stats count Sun, Moon and Star separately, and when hunting stops the log lists the session totals.
- Alchemy can play a sound when the target + is reached (off by default), with your own sound file of up to 1 MB if you like. The file stays in this browser and is never sent anywhere.
