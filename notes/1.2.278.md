# A full bag now says why the town trip stops
- When the character bag is nearly full and nothing in it can be sold or stored, the trip now warns you on arrival in town. It still sells the pet's loot, then stops before walking back, as before.
- The stop message now explains why: selling pet loot does not free bag space, because the loot only passes through one bag slot. It also tells you what to change: turn on "Sell ordinary gear from the character bag too", add Sell or Store item rules, or clear some bag space.
- With bag selling already on, it reminds you that only ordinary Chinese / European gear sells from the bag without a Sell rule.
> Only the messages changed; the trip itself works as before. Tested with the integration suite in simulation, not in a live game.
