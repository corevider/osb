# Alchemy queue and blue stones
- The Alchemy tab now has a queue. Add several items from your bag; the bot finishes one item, then starts the next.
- Each item goes Plus, then White, then Blue. Blues come last because a white stone that works can roll a blue again.
- Every item has its own goals, in three tabs: Plus (target +, elixir, powder, Luck/Steady/Immortal/Astral), Attribute (white stat %) and Blue.
- New: blue stones. Pick a goal per blue: add it, a value or more, or max. Only blues the item can take are shown, with the stones you have.
- The tab shows free magic slots. Luck, Steady, Immortal and Astral use the same slots, so a blue that does not fit is passed over.
- Out of materials: the queue goes to the next item, or stops if you untick that.
- New settings: delay between attempts, and a sound when the queue is done.
- Queued items are kept in the bag on town trips, like the selected alchemy item was.
> Tested with the integration and browser suites in simulation, not in a live game. Blue stone results on the live server are not checked yet.
