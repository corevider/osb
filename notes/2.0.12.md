# Emergency skills, safety triggers, a polygon hunting area and preferred monsters

- Emergency skills: Combat → Skills has up to 6 emergency skills. Pick a learned skill and a trigger: HP below a level, MP below a level, or at least N monsters attacking you. When the trigger holds and the skill is ready, it is cast before your attack rotation and buffs, while hunting, luring, in the unique hunt and walking back. Off and empty by default.
- Safety triggers: the new Safety settings can stop the bot, or send it to town and stop it there, when it dies too many times, the party gets too small, another player hurts your character, or a unique appears close by. All are off by default. A safety stop is final: auto-start, "Start again when the bot stops by itself" and a reconnect do not start the bot again.
- Polygon hunting area: on the Map page, draw the area as 3 to 32 corners with "Add corner here" at your position or by clicking the map, then tick "Use a polygon area" (off by default). The bot then attacks, protects, loots and returns only for what is inside it, so it keeps off cliffs, rivers and a neighbouring spawn that a circle would include. "Chase distance" still limits how far it follows.
- A polygon with fewer than 3 corners or crossing edges is shown in red, and the hunt radius is used. Spot by level, leveling spots, following a party member and the unique hunt keep the circle. The hunting spot is still where the bot walks back to. The corners are saved with your hunting spot: they are in the full settings file, not in share codes.
- Preferred monsters: Combat → Hunt area has a new list for quest monsters and drop farming. Add up to 10 monsters, best first, from the ones in view or seen this session, or type part of a name. A listed monster is fought before one that is not listed, and a higher row before a lower one. Monsters hitting you still come first, and your filters (classes, level range, names, hunting area, other players' targets) still apply.
- While fighting, the bot switches to a listed monster at least 2 rows higher than your target, at most once every 5 seconds, and never leaves a target that is hitting you. The list is empty by default and is part of share codes and backups.
