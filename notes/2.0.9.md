# Smarter town trips, zero-setup leveling with character builds, auto party invite and your own SoX sound

- Town trips walk only to the services that have something to do. A trip with only gear to buy no longer visits the warehouse, and a trip with nothing left goes straight back.
- A merchant whose saved standing spot is blocked (inside a forge, behind a fence) is reached from another spot beside it, and the spot that works is saved.
- A completely full bag no longer stops the trip when the bag holds something for the warehouse: the warehouse goes first and makes room.
- With "Walking back may use teleports and ferries (beta; pays their fee)" on, walking back across a river takes the ferry when the walk would go the long way round, or has no way at all.
- "Wear better gear" now swaps a weapon only for one of the same type.
- Zero-setup leveling picks spots for new characters and skips spots it cannot reach, buys gear for your own race and sex (a whole tier in one trip once all of it is open), replaces weak Seal gear but keeps it in the bag, keeps the weapon type you started with, always walks back and never buys reverse scrolls, and lets a summoned pickup pet collect the drops. When a town's gear runs out it moves on to the next town (Jangan, then Donwhang, then Hotan) and makes that town's statue your return point.
- New on the Setup page: "Character build". Leave it on Auto, or pick Glaive nuker, Blade tank, Bow nuker, Wizard, Cleric and more. Leveling never uses skills that root your character, such as Crystal Wall and Fire Wall.
- New on the Party page, off by default: "Auto-invite players". List the characters to invite and the bot invites them while they are in view; "Also invite anyone nearby" invites everyone within a radius you set. Only the party leader invites, never into a full party, never while the character is dead, and never on an account that joins a party leader. A player who does not answer is asked again after 30 seconds, then 1, 2, 4 minutes and so on, up to 10 minutes.
- Your own sound for SoX drops: "Choose SoX sound…" on the Loot page takes an audio file of up to 1 MB. It stays in this browser only and is never sent anywhere. "Clear SoX sound" brings back the built-in chime. The Star, Moon and Sun switches still decide which drops play it.
