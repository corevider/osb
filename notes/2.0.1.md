# Protect your party members

- New on the Party page: Protect party members. When a monster hits a member you ticked, the bot attacks that monster.
- Tick single members or All party members. Members who left stay in the list, greyed out.
- It works within a distance you choose (40 m by default) and inside your hunting area, and never attacks players.
- Your potions, death and return rules and town trips come first. Monsters in Skip these mobs and monsters other players fight are left alone.
- With Also with Attack monsters off, a support character protects the party too.
