# Unique hunt, buff weapon changes that wait for the pet, fewer repeated warnings

- New Unique hunt on the Setup page. Start it and the bot patrols for uniques, fights only uniques with your Unique rotation, berserk, buffs and potions, collects the unique's drops first and then stops. Your hunting spot, monster classes and rotations stay as they are; Combat → Unique hunt has all the settings.
- The patrol walks your own named spots, and with "Patrol the game's unique nests" also the places where the uniques you tick spawn, read from the game's own data, nearest first and within a distance you choose (1000 m by default). The bot can also remember where it saw uniques. When the game announces that a unique appeared, the patrol goes to its spots first, and the map rings the chosen nests.
- When the unique it went for dies, a new "Unique killed" chime plays (on by default, with a Test button), and the log and Session stats count it. The bot leaves a unique alone when other players have already brought it below 75% HP (you can change this), and gives up when others clearly out-damage you. A unique that hits you is always fought back.
- A buff weapon change no longer stops hunting with "Equipment move unconfirmed" while the pickup pet or another action keeps the game's inventory busy. A weapon change does not start while a pet pickup is on its way, and the pet waits while a weapon change runs. When the game refuses a weapon move and nothing moved, the bot sends it once more; if it is refused again, the log says so and hunting goes on unless no weapon is worn.
- A drop the game refuses to give your pet twice is given up until it disappears.
- "Manual control: resurrection travel cancelled." is logged only when a walk to a corpse is really cancelled, and the Activity log notes which game command started a manual-control pause.
