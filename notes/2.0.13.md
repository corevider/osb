# Monster classes with Prefer, Ignore or Avoid, and a safe start with the secondary weapon worn

- Monster classes: Combat → Hunt area → Monster classes now lists nine classes, from the weakest to the strongest: Normal, Champion, Party normal, Giant, Party champion, Party giant, Elite, Unique and Titan. Titan has its own choice and still uses the Unique rotation.
- Each class has three choices. Prefer: the bot takes it before any monster of an Ignore class. Ignore: the usual order; the bot takes it when no preferred class is available. Avoid: the bot never goes for it, and fights it only while it attacks you.
- The order: monsters hitting you come first (order Attackers), whatever their class; then your Preferred monsters list; then Prefer before Ignore; then the usual order. The bot never leaves a fight for a preferred class, and your other filters still apply. The lure's taunt follows the choices too.
- Your old settings carry over: a ticked class becomes Ignore and an unticked one Avoid, and the old Party switch sets all three party classes. Share codes and backups carry the choices.
- A character that enters the game holding its secondary weapon now puts the primary weapon on first, then buffs and hunts as usual, instead of stopping. The bot waits about 5 s after entering the game before it changes weapons, and a weapon change the game does not take is tried again after 30 s while hunting goes on with what is worn.
- Arrows and bolts no longer count as a shield, a reconnect or a revival is no longer refused while the combat gear goes back on, and the same weapon change runs at most twice a minute.
- With the chosen primary weapon neither worn nor in the bag (for example in the warehouse or the pet), hunting now stops after about 10 s and says what to do: put it in the bag, or choose another primary weapon (or None) under Combat.
